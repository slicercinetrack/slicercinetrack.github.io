# About the project

Tracking a moving target, a tumor or an organ, is central to many computer-assisted interventions, but confirming that a tracking algorithm is reliable is hard without proper tooling. SlicerCineTrack gives researchers an intuitive visual environment to spot exactly where a tracking algorithm succeeds or drifts, so they can validate reliability, refine algorithms, and tune parameters with confidence.

SlicerCineTrack is developed by the **Laboratory for Translational Medicine** at **Toronto Metropolitan University** (Toronto, ON, Canada), and was evaluated by established experts in computer-assisted interventions and image-guided therapy.
